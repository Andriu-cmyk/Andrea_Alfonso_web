Art is my way of understanding and expressing the world. My name is Andrea Alfonso del Moral and, at 18 years old, I am in the midst of my training as a tattoo artist, exploring techniques and building my own artistic identity with consistency and sensitivity. This project reflects my evolution, my commitment to learning, and my passion for transforming ideas into meaningful pieces.

In this section of the page, I wanted to open up a little more and show who I really am behind the tattoos. Since I was little, I have always felt a very strong connection to art: painting, creating, and experimenting have been part of me for as long as I can remember. Over time, that need to express myself artistically has grown and evolved until it has become a fundamental part of my life.
Although tattooing is one of my main forms of expression, it is not the only one. I also paint and enjoy any discipline that allows me to create something from scratch. For me, art has no limits or fixed rules; it comes from imagination, intuition, and the way each person interprets what is in front of them.

I have always thought that stains do not ruin things. Neither clothes, nor walls, nor furniture.Everything depends on the perspective from which it is seen and on the ability to transform it into something different. Where some people see chaos or disaster, others of us are able to find new ideas, personality, and beauty. In the end, art also consists of that: turning the everyday into something unique.
