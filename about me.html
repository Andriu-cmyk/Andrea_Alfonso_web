Art is my way of understanding and expressing the world. My name is Andrea Alfonso del Moral and, at 18 years old, I am in the midst of my training as a tattoo artist, exploring techniques and building my own artistic identity with consistency and sensitivity. This project reflects my evolution, my commitment to learning, and my passion for transforming ideas into meaningful pieces. In this section of the page, I want to open up and tell you in depth about myself. Since I was very young, I have liked to paint, create, experiment. And now that I am older, I have developed it further. Apart from tattooing, I also draw, and do graffiti. Any form of staining and making art is welcome in me. I think that stains do not ruin clothes, furniture, walls, floors... You just have to know how to use them, look at them from different angles, and create magic with them. A place with a new style, an original piece of clothing, etc. Where some see disaster, others see new opportunities.
