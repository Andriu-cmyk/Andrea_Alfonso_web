Care of your tattoo

Properly caring for your tattoo is essential to ensure good healing and keep the design in perfect condition over time. During the first few days, it is important to follow a series of basic care steps that will help prevent infections, preserve the colors, and ensure that the skin recovers properly. Below, we provide you with some essential recommendations that you should keep in mind after getting a tattoo.

1

Clean the tattoo properly

Wash the area 2–3 times a day with warm water and a fragrance-free neutral soap. Dry it with gentle pats using paper or a clean towel, without rubbing.

2

Keep the skin well hydrated

Apply a specific cream for tattoos or recommended by your tattoo artist in thin layers. Avoid overdoing it so that the skin can breathe.

3

Don't touch it or scratch it

It is normal for it to itch or feel uncomfortable during healing, but scratching it can damage the design and cause infections.

4

Avoid sun exposure

Do not expose the tattoo to direct sunlight while it heals. Once healed, always use sunscreen to keep the colors vibrant.

5

No prolonged water

During the first few days, avoid swimming pools, the beach, long baths, or saunas, as excess moisture can affect healing.

6

Let it heal naturally

Do not pick at the scabs or the skins that form. These will fall off on their own, and removing them early can cause ink loss or marks.
